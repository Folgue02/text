# folgue02/text

Small text helpers on top of the string methods of the standard library.

- `casing`: title case that keeps small words lower case, sentence case, and splitting identifiers into words.
- `align`: fitting text to a width, cutting it when it's too long, and aligning columns.
- `wrap`: word wrapping with indentation and justification.
- `slug`: turning titles into URL-friendly slugs.
